Welcome to Iyunga Secondary School for a better tomorrow. Our school aims to provide quality education to students that will enable them to reach their goals.

About Us

Our team consists of skilled professionals with years of experience in technology and innovation.

Location: Situated in Iyunga ward, Mbeya City, near the TAZARA railway station.
Status: Government-owned, boys-only boarding school serving students up to Form VI.

Services

Advanced Level (Form V & VI): Features strong combinations focusing primarily on science and technology:

  • PCM: Physics, Chemistry, Advanced Mathematics
  • PCB: Physics, Chemistry, Biology
  • PMC: Physics, Advanced Mathematics, Computer Science

National Examinations: Operating under NECTA school registration ID S0112, the institution consistently registers students for both CSEE (Form IV) and ACSEE (Form VI) examinations.
